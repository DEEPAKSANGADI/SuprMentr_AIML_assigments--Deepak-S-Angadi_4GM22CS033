Five Uses of Face/Object Detection
1. 	Security & Surveillance
• 	Detect suspicious activity in public spaces.
• 	Identify unauthorized access in restricted areas.
2. 	Healthcare
• 	Monitor patient movements (e.g., fall detection in elderly care).
• 	Track medical instruments during surgery.
3. 	Retail & Marketing
• 	Analyze customer demographics and behavior.
• 	Detect product placement and stock levels on shelves.
4. 	Automotive
• 	Driver monitoring systems (detect drowsiness or distraction).
• 	Object detection for autonomous driving (pedestrians, vehicles, traffic signs).
5. 	Smart Devices & Applications
• 	Face unlock on smartphones.
• 	Object recognition in AR apps (e.g., identifying furniture or plants).

 Designed Solution: Driver Drowsiness Detection System
Problem
Road accidents often occur due to driver fatigue. A system is needed to detect drowsiness and alert the driver before an accident happens.
Solution Design
• 	Input: Real-time video feed from a camera mounted on the dashboard.
• 	Process:
• 	Use face detection to locate the driver’s face.
• 	Apply eye detection to monitor blinking frequency and eye closure duration.
• 	Track head position to detect nodding or slumping.
• 	Algorithm:
• 	If eyes remain closed for more than 2 seconds or blinking rate drops significantly → classify as drowsy.
• 	If head tilts forward or sideways beyond a threshold → classify as drowsy.
• 	Output:
• 	Trigger an audio alarm or seat vibration to wake the driver.
• 	Optionally, send a notification to connected devices (fleet management system, family app).
• 	Enhancements:
• 	Integrate with vehicle sensors (speed, lane departure).
• 	Use infrared cameras for night driving.
